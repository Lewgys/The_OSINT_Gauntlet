Briefing

Welcome to The OSINT Gauntlet. You will be investigating a malicious threat actor who has compromised an employee's system and left a note behind. Your job is to gather as much information on them as you can and answer each task's questions. Dig deeper than you think you need to.

TO WIN: you must complete ALL tasks and crack the secret code. Three code fragments, A1, B1 and C1, are hidden somewhere in the investigation, outside the regular tasks. Find all three, join them in order and enter them in the terminal:
secret A1-B1-C1

Players are ranked by how much they complete, then by fewest hints used, then by who finished first, so think twice before reaching for a hint.

RULES: Every answer can be found with passive OSINT. Do NOT use active techniques: no contacting the account holder and no brute-forcing. This web app is out of scope, so no source-diving, no OWASP Top 10 and no testing shenanigans. Logs are reviewed.

You are on your own for this one: organisers cannot answer questions or give help during the challenge. Good luck.

- Lewis G
